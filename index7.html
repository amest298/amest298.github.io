About Your Candidates - Te Tai Tonga

About Takuta:

Ko Tākuta Ferris tōku ingoa. I am an independent Member of Parliament for Te Tai Tonga, guided by a deep commitment to whakapapa, whānau, and the wellbeing of the people I serve. I am a descendant of Takitimu, Kurahaupō and Horouta waka. My whakapapa ties me to Kāi Tahu and Kāti Māmoe in Te Waipounamu, to Ngāti Kuia, Ngāti Apa and Rangitāne in Te Tauihu o Te Waka a Māui, and to Ngāti Kahungunu and Ngāti Porou. I was raised in the small coastal village of Pōrangahau, where I developed a close affinity to the land and the sea. I understand that it is this connection that grounds me in knowing who I am and where I come from — my identity, the land, and the ocean are one and the same. Grounded in te ao Māori, my leadership reflects the values of manaakitanga, rangatiratanga, and a belief that systems must work for our communities — not the other way around.



Takuta Ferris is an independent candidate for the Te Tai Tonga electorate.

About Lisa:

Ko Dr Lisa Te Morenga ahau. Ko Ngāpuhi, ko Ngāti Whātua Ōrākei, ko Te Uri o Hau, ko Te Rarawa ōku iwi. Kei Te Whanganui-a-tara au e noho ana. I’m a Professor of Māori Health and Nutrition with more than 20 years’ experience in public health research, teaching, and advocacy. I’ve lived in Te Tai Tonga for more than 30 years across Te Whanganui-a-tara, Otautahi and Otepoti. I’m deeply committed to advancing whānau and environmental wellbeing, equitable health and social outcomes, and transforming systems through kaupapa Māori, community‑led solutions. I’ve been at the forefront of public health advocacy for fair, just and effective policy as the co-chair of Health Coalition Aotearoa. I want to bring my skills and experience to Parliament to advocate for policies that protect the amazing environments of Te Waipounamu and Te Whanganui-a-tara. Our mokopuna and their whānau deserve to have warm, dry homes, sustainable jobs and enough to eat. This current Government won’t listen to the to the evidence or the people. The Green Party has the plan to put power where it belongs, with people and communities.



The Green Party candidate for the Te Tai Tonga Electorate is Dr Lisa Te Morenga.

About Mananui:

Whānau are being hit hard by the cost of living and cuts to essential services. I’m humbled to stand for Labour in Te Tai Tonga. Our people just want the basics – stable work that pays fairly, a home that’s affordable, and health services that address the needs of whānau. Our people also want Te Tiriti honoured and better protection of whenua, moana and communities. That’s the kaupapa I’ll be taking to Parliament, if elected in November. I have extensive experience working alongside iwi, hapū and community organisations, particularly in environmental management and rangatahi development both here in Aotearoa and overseas. Join me and help be part of the campaign to take Te Tai Tonga back for Labour in November and make this a one term National Government. This election will be won in communities on the ground - one conversation at a time.



The Labour Party candidate for the Te Tai Tonga Electorate is Mananui Ramsden.

About Lisa Marie:

Born and raised in Murihiku, Lisa brings lived experience, education, service, and a deep commitment to whānau Māori across Te Waipounamu. A young Māori mother at sixteen, Lisa knows firsthand the realities many whānau face. Her journey from cleaning and baking, to becoming a head baker, bilingual primary teacher, kaupapa Māori educator, and specialist across Murihiku and Ōtepoti schools, is grounded in resilience and service. “I stand because Te Waipounamu deserves strong, visible Māori representation grounded in lived experience, education, and commitment to our people,” said Murch. “My journey has taught me that education, identity, whakapapa, and belonging can transform lives. I am standing to fight for the future our tamariki and whānau deserve.”.



The Te Pati Maori candidate for the Te Tai Tonga Electorate is Lisa Marie Murch.
