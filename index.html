Welcome to On A Roll!

On A Roll is an app designed to educate young people in Invercargill and Southland about the New Zealand Voting System in preperation for the 2026 General Election.

The general elecetion is a time for eligible people to vote on who they think will best run the country.

This year, the General Election will be held on November 7th.
