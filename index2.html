Enrolment and Voting

Find out whether you will be old enough to vote in this year's General Election!








Even if you won't be old enough to vote in the 2026 General Election, it is still a good idea to enrol to vote now. You will automatically be enrolled to vote once you turn 18, and you won't have to worry about it later!


Why do I need to enrol to vote?

Enrolling to vote means that you are registered to vote in this Election. Think of it as buying tickets to a concert. If you don't have the tickets, you can't get in to the concert. If you aren't enrolled to vote in this Election, you won't be able to vote.

How do I enrol to vote?

To enrol to vote, you can head to the Electoral Commision website and enrol online. It will take about 10 minutes and you will ned to have or need to create a RealMe identity account.
