What is a policy?

Policy can refer to an issue that parties debate, and a party's stance on said issue. Some common Policy topics this year are the cost of living, the environment, and tax rates. Each party can publish each of their own policies under these topics

Policy.nz is a great website to compare different parties stances on the issues that are being discussed this election. Head to policy.nz to explore, or use the viewport below!
